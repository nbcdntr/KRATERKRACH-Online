2. Daten und Zwecke / Categories and purposes

  • Gerät / Device: Local profile, credits, upgrades, tank selection, gameplay preferences, sound preference, server address and a random device identifier are held in browser localStorage or local app storage. These data help remember settings and identify the browser to the match service.
  • Spielername / Username: The browser sends a randomly generated 32-character hexadecimal device identifier when establishing the WebSocket connection. The server stores a SHA-256 hash of that identifier, an automatically assigned immutable nickname and the registration timestamp in a local SQLite file. No real name or email is requested in the game interface. As the identifier is transmitted in a connection URL, it can technically appear in intermediary connection logs.
  • Gefechte / Matches: Room code, pseudonym, tank class, alliance request, commands, shooting, position, health and match state are shared with other participants in the same private room; current room state exists in server memory.
  • Hosting: IP addresses, user-agent information, timestamps and technically necessary network logs may be processed by the hosting provider to serve the website and protect the systems.

3. Rechtsgrundlagen / Legal bases

Data strictly necessary to offer the requested multiplayer game and player identity are processed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where necessary for performance of the user service relationship. Technical security and abuse prevention rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (reliable and safe service operation). Storage or reading of information on a user's device follows § 25(2)(2) TDDDG insofar as it is strictly necessary for requested functionality. Optional analytics or advertising storage would require separate assessment and, where applicable, consent.

4. Empfänger und Ausland / Recipients and international transfers

The KRATERKRACH website and live multiplayer server are operated on Render Services, Inc., a US-based hosting provider. The deployed web service is configured for the Frankfurt, Germany region. This does not guarantee that all processing remains within the EU; Render's Data Processing Addendum states that processing and international transfers, including to the USA, may take place. The DPA provides for relevant safeguards including EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable; see Render DPA and Render trust/subprocessors.

No advertising, tracking analytics, payment provider or social-login SDK has been intentionally integrated in the current KRATERKRACH code.

5. Aufbewahrung und Löschung / Retention and erasure

Rooms exist in server memory for up to approximately two hours after creation, or until their host leaves or the server restarts. Locally stored game data remain until deleted in browser/app storage. A database record containing a hash of the device identifier, the nickname and creation time is held by the game's local SQLite file; the current Render Free instance uses a non-persistent file system, so the registry can be erased on restart, spin-down or deployment. Permanent global nickname retention cannot therefore be guaranteed in this beta.

For hosting connection logs: Only for the period necessary for technical security and troubleshooting in accordance with the actual hosting provider settings. Please contact the controller to obtain the applicable retention criteria.

Players may contact the controller to request access or erasure of any identifiable stored records. The beta currently has no in-game self-service erasure control; for that reason, identity management is subject to further development prior to open public release.

6. Rechte / Your rights

To the extent applicable under the GDPR, data subjects may request information, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. A data subject may also complain to a competent data protection authority. Direct requests to the contact email above. Because accounts are pseudonymous, appropriate proof of control over the device identifier may be needed; please do not publish private identifiers.

7. Minderjährige / Children

KRATERKRACH is in a family-test phase and has no official age rating. Guardians should supervise use by minors. In Germany, where consent is the relevant GDPR legal basis for a service offered directly to a child, consent for users under 16 must be given or authorised by their guardian in accordance with Article 8 GDPR. This is not a blanket requirement for every processing operation that relies on another valid legal basis.

8. Technically necessary storage / Technisch notwendige Speicherung

The current game uses localStorage, an offline service worker and other technically functional browser storage to remember game operation and preferences. No optional analytics or marketing cookies are included in the reviewed code. If additional cookies, ads or tracking tools are added, this notice and the consent management must be reassessed.

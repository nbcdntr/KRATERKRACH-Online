## Complete before publishing beyond a private family beta

1. Edit **web/legal.html** and **web/privacy.html**. Enter the **full legal provider name**, a **real serviceable street address** (or a properly appointed service-of-process address), and **working email plus a fast contact option**. Keep **Created by T. Gligor** as the author credit. Mirror both pages into `android/app/src/main/assets/` (or use the provided included versions and update both).
2. Replace the privacy policy placeholders with the actual host company, jurisdiction, processors and data transfer safeguards. Enter retention/deletion procedure for generated usernames and device IDs.
3. Complete data deletion functionality and set a proportionate identity-record retention policy. The free Render file system is **ephemeral**, so worldwide permanent nickname uniqueness is **not guaranteed** until you add persistent database hosting.
4. Verify multiplayer gameplay, alliance and damage balance, secure WebSockets, abuse controls, and the legal right to use all artwork, sounds, libraries and app name.
5. For a public release to minors, assess age classification/Google Play Families rules and parental permission requirements.
6. Review appropriate privacy/security measures, server access logging, GDPR Art. 13 disclosures, DDG/MStV provider identification and BGB liability language.
7. Test the /impressum, /datenschutz and game footer links from a phone.
